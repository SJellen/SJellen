## Research Programs

### Universal Sports Graph

**Active Protocol Program**

The **Universal Sports Graph (USG)** is a protocol stack for representing sports broadcast rights as structured, interoperable data.

It defines a neutral machine-readable layer for:

- rights definition and ownership
- entitlement and access authorization
- registry-backed auditability
- settlement and clearinghouse primitives

The core architecture is:

**rights graph → entitlement enforcement → authoritative registry**

Published work includes:

- *The Universal Sports Graph — Blueprint Edition*
- RFC 0001 — Architecture
- RFC 0002 — Entitlement Token Profile
- RFC 0003 — Registry Architecture
- USG Registry
- resolver and reference tooling

Canonical protocol artifacts are maintained in:

**`sjellen/protocols`**

### The Shorter Working Life

**Active Research Program**

A research program examining whether advanced economies can reduce the amount of lifetime labor required from individuals without treating income replacement as the sole policy objective.

The work focuses on retirement, transition design, fiscal conditions, labor participation, and the portion of life released from mandatory full-time work.

Published artifacts include the core framework, implementation outline, transition simulation, fiscal conditions analysis, retirement mechanism, UBI comparison, and Free Phase memo.

---

### The Twenty-First Slav Epic

**Active Research Program**

A public-memory and institutional-art research project examining what a contemporary successor to Alphonse Mucha's *Slav Epic* might represent a century after its completion.

The project explores memory, sovereignty, war, displacement, common work, and the relationship between cultural inheritance and contemporary Europe.

## Selected Institutional Research

### Something for Something

*Capping Americans' Exposure to Healthcare Risk*

A healthcare and tax-system proposal centered on a simple guarantee: nationally covered medical care should have a finite annual limit on an individual's financial exposure.

The framework combines household-level liability limits, continued private insurance participation, public-program coordination, and a federal backstop.

## Protocol & Reference Infrastructure

The `protocols` repository contains the standards-oriented development surface of the Universal Sports Graph.

### Authoritative artifacts

- protocol specifications
- registry object schemas
- entitlement and settlement schemas
- canonical identifiers
- deterministic indexes
- registry metadata

### Reference tooling

- schema validation
- integrity verification
- digest checks
- index inspection
- resolver interfaces

Reference tooling demonstrates protocol behavior and inspectability. It is not presented as production infrastructure.

Authoritative state remains in the registry rather than the resolver or interface layer.
